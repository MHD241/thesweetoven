About The Sweet Oven

The Sweet Oven is a bakery in Dunfermline. We bake Welsh cakes and sell mini bullar, Swedish cinnamon buns, and we plan to add other sweet bakes over time.

Small batches

Everything is baked in small batches, so each cake gets the same attention.

Simple recipe

Our Welsh cakes are flour, butter, sugar, sultanas, egg, milk and cinnamon, cooked on a griddle.

Clear allergen info

Every pack comes with its ingredients and allergens listed, so you know what you are buying.
